From the field

What's happening, and where.

Short reports from the places we work — what shipped, what got built, and what changed for the people there.

Haiti

A 40-foot container, cleared and moving

Haiti continues to be a challenge, but the work there goes on: clean water and food for children. We recently finished shipping a 40-foot container, and it was picked up from customs right away — which almost never happens.

Inside was food and medical supplies. All of it went to three villages in the northern region hit by the earthquake, distributed on the ground by local church leaders who know exactly which families need what.

Kenya

Maisha Academy tops its zone — and its clinic goes solar

We continue to support Maisha Academy in Kenya. The school serves orphans and local families alike, and it is now being recognized as one of the best in its region. When the eighth graders sat their national exams, Maisha ranked first in our zone and fifth across Kisumu East.

The solar power we supplied for the new girls' dormitory does two things at once: it makes the dorm safer at night, and it lets the girls study after dark.

We also put solar on the three-year-old clinic, so vaccine refrigeration finally holds a steady temperature. That single change qualified the clinic for National Health Insurance Fund accreditation, which means villagers can now get affordable care there. For the Maisha Medical Clinic, it is a large step toward standing on its own.
