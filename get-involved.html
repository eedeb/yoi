Get involved

Pick up the phone. That's usually how it starts.

We are a small organization, so there is no committee to get past. Call, write or email any time — with a question, or to say you'd like to help.

Where we need help

Four programs, and room in each one.

Health

HIV and AIDS prevention and treatment

Supporting the testing, medication and ongoing care that keep people alive and keep the illness from spreading further.

Expertise

Consulting for mission and relief agencies

Thirty years of shipping, customs, clinic-building and field logistics. If your organization is trying to solve a problem we've already hit, we'll help.

Advocacy

Reaching people others have written off

Meeting the needs of people overlooked because of poverty or social status — the ones who don't appear on anyone's list.

Go

Short-term missions trips

Travel with us to the places we work. Medical training helps, but plenty of the work needs hands rather than credentials.

What a gift covers

Small numbers, because costs in the field are small.

This is roughly what your money buys once it reaches our outreach work in Africa.

$15 Medicine and food for one child Per month
$10 Vaccines for one child One-time
$20 Medical care for a child living with AIDS Per month, in addition to the above
$20 A water purification system for one family Per year
