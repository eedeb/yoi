Get involved

Pick up the phone. That's usually how it starts.

We are a small organization, so there is no committee to get past. Call, write or email any time — with a question, or to say you'd like to help.

Where help is needed

Four ways to stand with the ministries.

Each of the three ministries has told us what it needs most. Read their stories →

Give

Fund what they need most

About 96% of every gift goes straight to the ministries: rice for hungry students in Haiti, support for village teachers in Kenya, and Maisha’s new rural hospital until it can sustain itself.

Pray

Pray for them

Prayer is one of the three things we give every ministry, alongside donations and shipments. Pastor Gesner and his staff are teaching through gang violence in Haiti, and both ministries in Kenya are growing faster than their resources.

Go

Teach or volunteer

Grace Life Bible College welcomes pastors to come and teach for several weeks at a time. The Maisha Project needs volunteers for its camp programs and its two medical facilities. Get in touch and we can tell you more.

Sponsor

Sponsor a child

The Maisha Project’s school of 600 children offers sponsorships for individual children. Find out more at maishaproject.org.

What a gift covers

Small numbers, because the work is done by local people.

National leaders and their staff can do a great deal on very little. This is what a gift can do once it reaches them.

$3.90 Rice with vegetables and oil, three times a week, for one student or teacher at Institution Puits Jacob in Haiti Per month
$6 A day’s support for a Grace Life teacher taking the Bible to village churches in Kenya Per day
$2,500 Those meals for the whole school in Haiti: 600 students and 40 staff Per month
