History

YOI has been a 501(c)(3) since 1985. It was started by Dr. Richard Niemeyer, who had, along with his wife Jean, previously spent 15 years traveling to mission fields all over the world wherever there were emergency situations in need of a doctor. He also ran his own medical practice in Leola, PA. Then with the charity, they were able to expand their effectiveness in reaching people with medical help, medicines, clinics, AIDS test kits, food and supplies, along with preventive health education. Their purpose was to support the work of others who were spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ. No need was too challenging, whether it was for clean water, food, solar or other needs. He also sent many shipments of 40-foot containers filled with all kinds of critically needed supplies, as well as millions of nutritious rice packets. In addition, he aided in the production and distribution of a lifesaving product called The Peanut Butter Project, which saved the lives of countless malnourished children under 2 years old. His master’s in public health gave him access to government officials in various countries and their health departments to help plan preventive health programs.

Youth Outreach International (YOI) has served many people and organizations throughout the world for many years. Dr. Richard Niemeyer has dedicated himself to bringing good health, clean water, and food to help other organizations who believe in the same mission statement as YOI does. These other organizations are also highly dedicated to evangelism and discipleship. We have been particularly impressed with the capability and passion of the national Christians to carry out this mission with great efficiency, since they already know the culture, language and needs of their own people. Financially, they can operate on a much lower budget than outsiders. However, they still greatly appreciate and need outside help to expand their outreaches.
