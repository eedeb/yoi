Helping national Christians serve their own people.

Since 1985, Youth Outreach International has supported people spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ with medical help, clinics, clean water, food and supplies. Its founder, Dr. Richard Niemeyer, had already spent fifteen years with his wife Jean answering emergencies on mission fields around the world. Today we stand behind three ministries — two in Kenya and one in Haiti — led by national Christians who know their own people, and who can meet their needs on a much smaller budget than outsiders ever could.

How we work

Our purpose is to help, not to run things.

We have known each of these ministries for years, visiting them in their own countries and hosting their leaders in our home, and we have been so impressed by their capability, passion and vision. With each one, our purpose is not to run or control anything, but to help them do what God has called them to.

  • DonationsPassed on to the ministries: about 96% of every gift
  • Shipments40-foot containers of books, equipment and supplies
  • PrayerFor each ministry and the people it serves
  • VisitsTransportation, housing and speaking engagements to raise funds when their leaders come to the States
  • CounselA sounding board as God leads them to develop and expand their outreaches, with regular calls, texts and emails

The ministries

Three ministries we know well.

Two in Kenya and one in Haiti, each led by national Christians and their own staff, who know the language, customs and culture of the people they serve. Here is a brief introduction to each. Their full stories are well worth reading too.

Webuye, Kenya · Pastor Elyves Situma Grace Life Bible College A Bible college teaching theology, church music and computers, whose seminary graduates about twenty pastors a year. Its teachers also ride out by motorcycle to bring sound teaching to pastors and churches in remote villages. Read the full story Montrouis, Haiti · Pastor Gesner Institution Puits Jacob An accredited school of all twelve grades for about 600 children, founded in 2015. Gangs have reached the town, but the teachers keep teaching, and the school serves a plain rice meal three times a week to students who often arrive too weak to study. Read the full story Kenya · Beatrice Williams Maisha Project A certified school of 600 children with dorms for at-risk children, feeding, water and widows’ programs, and a clinic that has grown into a self-sustaining hospital. Who we are

What we're for, and how we go about it.

Our vision

Youth Outreach International is a non-denominational evangelical ministry that exists to facilitate worldwide evangelistic opportunities for both the underprivileged and the influential. By meeting medical, educational, social and spiritual needs, we yield improved health and changed lives through Christ.

Our mission

Youth Outreach International shares the love of Jesus in countries where we have relationships and influence, by supplying the best possible medical care, diagnostic equipment and pharmaceuticals. We will gather funds, coordinate transportation, and be instrumental in providing quality health education and disease prevention to all people we encounter.

Envision a world where all children are cared for, valued and loved. Your involvement can help make that vision a reality.
